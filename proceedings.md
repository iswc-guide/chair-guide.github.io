# Publishing the Proceedings

## Introduction

The **Proceedings & Metadata Chairs** are responsible for the coordination and assembly of the Proceedings Volumes, working primarily with the *Research Track Chairs* (who oversee the Research, Resources and In-Use tracks) and the *General Chair* (who authors the Preface). Other track chairs contribute where relevant.

The main volumes are currently published by [Springer](https://www.springer.com/) under an agreement with [SWSA](https://swsa.semanticweb.org), and Springer's dates are quite strict. Associated volumes (e.g. workshop proceedings) are typically published via [CEUR-WS](https://ceur-ws.org). **Do not underestimate the work involved in preparing the proceedings**: things need to be scheduled in a timely manner to ensure that the volumes are ready and that preprints of papers are available before the conference.

## Time Line

### Before the Notification phase

Agree on a **schedule of activities for preparing the proceedings** with the *Proceedings & Metadata Chairs*. The schedule should be anchored on the deadline by which the proceedings have to be ready (Springer's dates).

### At Notification

Ensure that the details for the final *Camera Ready Version* of the accepted papers are ready to share when the notifications are released. These details can accompany the notifications, or be sent out independently by the *Proceedings & Metadata Chairs*, but ensure that they are sent in a timely manner — turnaround can be tight.

Note that SWSA has an agreement with Springer whereby:

1. **preprints** of papers can be published on the conference website;
2. the official proceedings papers are **available for up to two weeks after the conference**.

### Up to the conference

Coordinate with the *Proceedings & Metadata Chairs* on the assembly of the volumes, ensuring that the Preface and accompanying material are ready in time for Springer's deadline.

---

## The Preface

The Preface is authored by the *General Chair*; delegate the summaries of each track to the relevant track chairs. The Preface should include:

- **submission and acceptance rates** for the different tracks;
- **information about sponsors**;
- a **list of main track PCs** (ensure that the name/affiliation data is correct — give PC members a chance to update theirs before the list is locked);
- an **entry for each Keynote**, including title, author and abstract.

---

## Workshop Proceedings

Workshop proceedings are typically published via [CEUR-WS](https://ceur-ws.org), and are coordinated separately by the *Workshops and Tutorials Chairs* in conjunction with the individual workshop organisers. The *General Chair* should be aware of CEUR's submission requirements and ensure that the timeline does not clash with the Springer deadlines for the main volumes.

---

## Notes, Tips and useful Resources

* **TIP**: Communicate the camera-ready instructions to authors **at the same time** as notifications go out. Late communication is the most common cause of slipped Springer deadlines.

* **TIP**: The Preface is often the last thing the *General Chair* writes, but the data it contains (submission numbers, acceptance rates, PC lists, sponsor lists, keynote titles/abstracts) needs to be gathered from across the OC. Start collecting it early.

* **TIP**: For PC name/affiliation correctness, run a final pass with the *Research Track Chairs* a couple of weeks before the Preface is finalised — names and affiliations change.
