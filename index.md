# Congratulations on being General Chair of ISWC\!

The aim of this guide is to aid in the running of the conference, and to pass on best practice.  Therefore it should be seen as a living (evolving) document to aid future chairs\! Although the guide is not meant to be a complete step-by-step instruction manual, it has been reorganised to provide direction and advice regarding the different aspects of the conference organisation, including a summary of things that might not be obvious, or that you should watch out for.

The guide has been structured to cover the different phases and activities, and is not meant to be prescriptive.  Each section includes a list of *notes, tips and resources* that should be updated each year, to ensure that the guide is always up to date and is useful.

## Overview

As *General Chair*, you’ll probably have been invited two years prior to the conference date. Typically, nominations for **hosting the conference** (together with the **dates of the conference itself**) are submitted to [SWSA](https://swsa.semanticweb.org) two years in advance, typically with the proposer as the **Local Chair** (that heads the local organisation team). Once the decision has been made, the **General Chair** should coordinate to clarify the roles that each will take.  Details of these roles can be found in the section on the [Organising Committee](organising-committee.html).

## Guide Structure

The guide is organised around the lifecycle of running ISWC, roughly in the order things need to happen:

1. **[Organising Committee](organising-committee.html)** — forming the OC, role definitions, OC sizing, conflicts of interest, awards.
2. **[Local Organisers](local-organiser.html)** — the venue, accommodation, travel, social events, budget, registration pricing, free/reduced registrations.
3. **[Managing EasyChair](Easychair-CFP.html)** — the submission and review system, CFP schedule, invited speakers.
4. **[PC & SPC](pc-and-spc.html)** — the two-tiered Programme Committee, the SPC meeting, PC invitation.
5. **[Post-Notification Planning](post-notification.html)** — what the *General Chair* does between notification and the conference itself.
6. **[Proceedings](proceedings.html)** — Springer, the Preface, camera-ready, workshop proceedings.
7. **[Before the Conference](prior-conference.html)** — the final-month checklist.
8. **[During the Conference](conference.html)** — running the event, recurring meetings, awards on the day.
9. **[Post-Conference](post-conference.html)** — wrap-up.
10. **[Publicity](publicity.html)** — website, mailing lists, advertising.
11. **[Timeline](timeline.html)** — a single-glance summary of all the milestones, linked to the pages above.

Each page ends with a **Notes, Tips and useful Resources** section that should be updated by each year's OC.
