# Timeline of preparing the conference

This page is a single-glance overview of the milestones across the two years of preparation. Each item links to the relevant page for detail.

---

## 18+ months

- **Bid for the conference** (venue, dates) submitted to SWSA by the prospective *Local Chairs*. See [Local Organisers](local-organiser.html).
- *General Chair* identified by SWSA.

## 12–18 months

- **Finalise the OC**: invite and confirm all main chair positions in time for the website launch and OC Lunch at the prior ISWC. See [Organising Committee](organising-committee.html).
- **Begin website preparation** (private). See [Publicity](publicity.html).
- **Procure Easychair** licence. See [Managing EasyChair](Easychair-CFP.html).

## 12 months — at the prior ISWC

- *General Chair* (or representative) attends **Town Hall**, **OC Lunch** and **Closing Ceremony** at the prior ISWC. See [Organising Committee](organising-committee.html).
- **Launch website** immediately after the closing session of the prior ISWC. See [Publicity](publicity.html).
- Initiate publicity campaign — website, dates, location.

## One week after the prior ISWC

- *General Chair* organises **Handover meeting** with previous and new OC. See [Organising Committee](organising-committee.html#handover).
- Incoming track chairs schedule **one-to-one calls** with their predecessors.

## 6–12 months

- **Configure Easychair**: email aliases, submission forms, review forms. See [Managing EasyChair](Easychair-CFP.html).
- **Define and publish CFPs** for each track; coordinate the submission/notification date schedule.
- **Invite SPC then PC** (in that order). See [PC & SPC](pc-and-spc.html).
- **Sponsor outreach** begins around the main-track deadlines: agree Sponsor Levels, contact companies. See [Local Organisers](local-organiser.html#registration-pricing-and-budget).
- **Identify candidate keynote speakers** (run by SWSA before inviting).

## Notification phase (~5–6 months)

- **Release notifications** for the main tracks.
- **Publish registration** at the same time as notifications. See [Local Organisers](local-organiser.html#free-and-reduced-registrations) and [Post-Notification Planning](post-notification.html).
- **First call for Student Travel Grants** goes out with (or before) notifications.
- **Camera-ready instructions** issued. See [Proceedings](proceedings.html).

## ~3 months

- Begin **overall schedule planning** for all five days. See [Post-Notification Planning](post-notification.html#planning-the-overall-schedule).
- **Videolectures negotiation** for recording. See [Post-Notification Planning](post-notification.html#recording).

## ~1 month

- **Opening / Closing Ceremony slides** and **Town Hall** preparation begins.
- Assign **Session Chairs** for keynotes and main-track sessions. See [Before the Conference](prior-conference.html).
- Confirm **award certificates** design and signatories. See [Awards](organising-committee.html#awards).

## At the conference

- Run the event. See [During the Conference](conference.html).
- Recurring meetings to plan for: SWSA dinner, JWS EB lunch (Day 1), Mentoring lunch (Day 2), next-year OC lunch (Day 3). See [During the Conference](conference.html#recurring-meetings-at-the-conference).

## Post-conference

- **Online presentation** of the conference to SWSA, jointly with *Local Chairs*. See [Post-Conference](post-conference.html).
- Tie up loose ends — expenses documentation, award payments, etc.
