## Post-conference

* A week or two after the conference, prepare to present an **overview of the conference** (along with the *Local Chairs*) to SWSA online.
* There will probably still be a few loose ends waiting to be tied up: attendees who need documentation to claim expenses, **award payments** that need to be fulfilled, **proceedings** finalisation (in particular, the two-week embargo window for the official Springer versions — see [Proceedings](proceedings.html)), Springer/CEUR sign-offs, and outstanding sponsor invoices. These details tend to be relatively minor, but should not be left to drift.
* Coordinate with the **next year's OC** on the handover of materials (Easychair settings export, sponsor contacts, mailing-list templates, sample budget). See the [OC handover section](organising-committee.html#handover).
* Hand over the **best/worst PC and SPC feedback** to next year's *Research Track Chairs* (informal — SWSA does not maintain such lists; see the [PC & SPC page](pc-and-spc.html)).
* Update the public spreadsheets of past **Organising Committees** and **keynote speakers**. See the [OC page](organising-committee.html#notes-tips-and-useful-resources).
* Enjoy some hobbies, friends, family, etc.
