## Leading up to the conference

This page is the **final-month checklist**. By this point, the work owned by the [Local Organisers](local-organiser.html) (venue, AV, catering, badges) and the [Proceedings](proceedings.html) team should be substantially complete; the *General Chair*'s job is to verify and chase.

* In the couple of months prior to the conference, there will be a lot of work for the **Web Chairs** adding details of accepted papers, PC, programme, etc., to the website, as well as sending messages via social media. Prepare them for this (and of course, get as much as possible on the website as early as possible). See [Publicity](publicity.html).
  * **TIP**: With more details fixed (keynotes, panels, etc.), this is also a good time to encourage **local attendees** to attend. A nice-looking brochure with the conference's key selling points, sent on local mailing lists, is an effective approach.
* Double-check that all is going well with **sponsors** (perks are being met), with the **student travel grants**, etc. Check through all OC roles to ensure the final preparations are in place.
* Keep an eye on **workshops** to check attendee numbers, and cross-check with the *Workshops and Tutorials Chairs* that all events will go ahead, and their duration. Otherwise this may require re-planning.
* Figure out with all *Track Chairs* a **strategy for no-shows**. Note that while authors may reach out in advance, some might only be "no-shows" on that day.
* Chat with the *Local Chairs* to review details of the venue. Make sure it will be clear (via obvious signs) what sessions are in which place, and how to get there. Also double-check that nothing critical depends on one particular student volunteer, and if they have a key role, that they will be given proper instructions or induction. Be sure that **sponsor logos** will be presented appropriately at the start of keynotes, etc.
  * **TIP**: Companies are picky about how their logos are used: they want particular logos, their permission to display logos needs to be sought, they might ask for promo material, they don't like their logos on anything disposable, etc. Check with them how their logo will be used! See also [Local Organisers](local-organiser.html#badges-programme-and-goody-bag).
* Confirm with the *Local Chairs* **what will be given to attendees**: a bag, a programme, etc.
  * **TIP**: Something many people at ISWC 2024 appreciated was having printed programmes. Aside from that, a lanyard, a badge, and wifi details if needed, there is not much need for anything else (a nice tote bag is okay, but many of us already have dozens of these).
* From experience, it may also be worth checking with the *Local Chairs* that the conference venue is **not double-booked with a wedding**.
* About a **month before the conference** is a good opportunity to start thinking about **Opening Ceremony slides**, **Closing Ceremony slides**, and the **Town Hall**. This requires a lot of coordination between you, SWSA, the *Local Chairs*, the *Track Chairs*, and next year's *General/Local Chairs*. Figure out who presents what.
  * **TIP**: Google Slides is very useful for collaboratively editing these decks.
* Also about a month before the conference is a good time to assign **Session Chairs** for everything: keynotes, main tracks, etc. Keynotes are typically chaired by *Research Track Chairs*, a *Local Chair*, or yourself. Other track chairs, SPC members, etc., can be invited to chair Main Track sessions. Be sure to send them instructions on how to chair the session (arrive W minutes before, talks are X minutes + Y minutes Q&A, what to do if they cannot make it, what to do if there is a no-show, etc.).
* Ensure that all accepted papers are available via the homepage prior to the conference. See [Proceedings](proceedings.html).
* Double-check that **all papers have an author registered**, and follow up if necessary.
* Put together a [design for award certificates](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/10LhO02k-xY54cG5e1I7ppzwdtoRGSPmP/edit?usp=drive_link&ouid=107368889730418788483&rtpof=true&sd=true) and coordinate with *Track Chairs* to have all possible awards that will be needed ready to print. Think also about who will be able to sign them, and when (if the corresponding *Track Chairs* don't attend, the *General Chair* can sign). See [Awards](organising-committee.html#awards).
* Prepare for those who cannot attend or don't show: request videos, slides, remote connection, etc.
